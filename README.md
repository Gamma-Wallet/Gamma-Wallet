**Gamma Wallet** is a cardless, multi-service digital wallet for local businesses and their customers. Every reward is a **reward contract** — a promise of value between a business and its customer, recorded and verifiable, never money and never crypto.

Its heart is **Community Loyalty**: the more customers take part, the more everyone's reward grows — steady demand for the business, without discounting.

- **Customers** keep every reward, credit and membership in one app, and show one QR code in every shop.
- **Businesses** run their loyalty from Gamma Business, the web portal and the Retailer app — register and start on the free tier.
